## 1. App shell (`FieldlineApp`, `div.app` / `div.app.tech-app`)
- Red inline banner "Changes are not being saved" when `store.storageWarning`.
- `header.top`: `.brand` (b "Fieldline" + small "Teams" or "Tech"); Company `<select>` (orgs: "Harbor Coast Mechanical", "Second company (empty)"; hidden in tech mode); **Signed-in `<select>`** (users of the org — this is the role switcher: the selected user's role drives everything: Admin, Supervisor, Dispatcher, Customer service, Billing, Tech); `.grow`; "Reset demo" `.ghost` (window.confirm).
- `nav.nav` tab buttons (`.on`), `.nav-count` red badge on Follow-ups = overdue waits + due quote follow-ups + lump-sum PO reminders. Tabs by role: **Admin** all 13 (Dispatch board, Follow-ups, New WO, Customers, Tech status, Timesheets, Work orders, Quoting, Purchasing, Inventory, Billing, New customer, Users); **Dispatcher/Supervisor** all except Purchasing, Billing, New customer, Users; **Customer service** New WO, Customers, New customer; **Billing** Billing + Work orders (closed only); **Tech** no tabs → `TechView`.
- Nav state: `view` + `navStack[{label, view, scroll}]`, used only for Purchasing hops (Issue PO / open PO from a WO). Origin screen stays mounted as `main.screen-held` (`data-nav-screen`) so scroll restores; `popNav` returns. Tab click clears stack. `main.main` variants `.main-day` (board), `.follow-main`.
- **BackBar** (`.back-bar`): `.ghost` "Back to {place}"; when `editing`, `.back-bar-save` with "Save" ghost + "Save and back" primary; `dirty` → confirm "Discard your changes?". Places seen: Dispatch board, Work orders, Customers, Quoting, Purchasing, Billing, Inventory, Timesheets, Break exceptions, "the bill", "my day", "my WOs", "lookup", "Work order 1042-2", site/customer names, "Invoice 7", report titles.
- Overlays: `.call-scrim > section.call-window[role=dialog]` (WO detail); `.new-call-scrim > .new-call-sheet` (New WO on board, Equipment sheet `.equipment-sheet`, PM contract); `.history-call` (`CallHost` wrapper for WO opened from Customers / New WO / inventory report); `.verify-dialog` (tech Complete). Escape closes CallWindow/NewWorkOrder with dirty confirm.
- No toasts; feedback is inline `.hint` text ("Saved", "Sent to the office for review.") and `window.confirm`. **No command parser in the UI**: `store.applyCommand` exists in `lib/store.tsx` but nothing calls it (README's `Close, 1.5 hrs, filter change` feature has no entry point). Timers: 15 s tick for overdue, 1 s for live clocks.
